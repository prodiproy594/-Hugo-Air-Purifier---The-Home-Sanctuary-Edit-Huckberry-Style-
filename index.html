Field Notes: Indoor Air Quality & Thermal Synergy

01 • Air Turnover & Dead-Zone Mitigation

Eliminating Stagnant Pockets in High-Traffic Spaces

Square box purifiers push air strictly in one direction, leaving corners of living spaces stagnant. Hugo’s cylindrical 360° intake draws air from all elevations, producing an acoustic convection vortex that turns over the room volume efficiently without high fan drag.

02 • Kitchen Off-Gassing & Wildfire Season

Targeting Fine VOCs and Invisible Cooking Aerosols

High-heat searing and exterior seasonal smoke release ultra fine carbon residues that settle invisibly into the air and onto fabrics. Activating Hugo on high for 20 minutes post-cooking absorbs these particulates and volatile compounds before they embed into furniture.
